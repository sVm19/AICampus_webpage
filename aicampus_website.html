FAQ

Common Questions

Is AICampus free to use?

AICampus offers a free tier with access to introductory lessons. A premium subscription unlocks the full course library, projects, and certificates.

Do I need programming experience?

No! Our curriculum takes complete beginners from zero to building real AI projects. Advanced tracks are available for experienced developers too.

What topics does AICampus cover?

Machine Learning fundamentals, Deep Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, Generative AI (LLMs, diffusion models), Python for AI, and Data Science.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account inside the app, or visit aicampus.com/delete-account. All data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Is my payment information secure?

All payments are processed through Google Play's secure infrastructure. We never store your payment details on our servers.
